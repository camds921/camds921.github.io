Privacy Policy — Stamp Duty Australia

Effective date: 26 June 2026 App: Stamp Duty Australia (au.lowkey.stampduty) Publisher: Lowkey Labs (Camillo de Silva), Australia Contact: camds921@gmail.com

Lowkey Labs ("we", "us") built Stamp Duty Australia ("the app") to be useful and private. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app does and does not handle. We have written it to reflect exactly how the app actually works.

The short version

  • We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email, address, phone number, or any account.
  • The figures you enter (price, deposit, state, etc.) are processed on your device and stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to any server.
  • We do not run any analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking of our own.
  • We show ads through Google AdMob, and offer an optional paid upgrade through the App Store / Google Play. Those services handle some data as described below.
  • You can remove ads entirely with the one-time "Pro" purchase.

Information you enter

The app is a calculator. Property prices, deposits, loan terms, buyer type, and the scenarios you save are stored locally on your device so you can return to them. This information stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is deleted if you delete the app. We never see it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app displays ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, AdMob may collect and process certain information, which can include your device's advertising identifier, general (non-precise) location inferred from your IP address, device type and settings, and ad-interaction data. AdMob uses this to display and measure ads and, depending on your choices, to personalise them.

  • On iOS, the app uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency: you will be asked whether to allow tracking, and your choice is respected.
  • Where required, we show a Google consent message so you can choose between personalised and non-personalised ads.
  • You can remove all ads — and therefore all ad-related data collection by AdMob within the app — by buying Pro (a one-time purchase).

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

The optional "Pro" upgrade (and any future in-app purchases) are processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your card details. The store tells us only whether a purchase succeeded, so the app can unlock Pro. Refunds and billing are handled by Apple or Google under their terms.

Links to partners

The app may show links such as "Compare home loans" that open in your browser. If you tap one, you leave the app and are taken to a third party (for example, a comparison or broker site). We may earn a referral fee if you proceed. We do not pass your entered figures or any personal information to those partners; tapping a link simply opens their website, where their own privacy policy applies. We do not give financial advice.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect personal information.
  • We do not have user accounts or logins.
  • We do not run our own servers that receive your data.
  • We do not use Firebase, analytics, crash reporting, or any first-party tracking.
  • We do not sell data. (We have no personal data to sell.)

Children

The app is intended for adults making property decisions. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Security

Because your information stays on your device, the most important protection is your device itself. We recommend using a device passcode and keeping your operating system up to date.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop ad data collection: decline tracking (iOS), choose non-personalised ads, or buy Pro to remove ads.
  • Delete everything: delete the app to remove all locally stored data.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (and the Australian Privacy Principles), the EU/UK GDPR, or the California CCPA. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is generally nothing for us to retrieve or delete on our side — but you can contact us at any time with questions.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this policy and its effective date, and where appropriate note the change in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email camds921@gmail.com and we'll respond.

This policy is provided for transparency and store compliance and does not constitute legal advice.
